# How I think and work

## My path into AI

I started in Electrical and Computer Engineering in Porto. An image classification project there got me interested in AI, and I moved to Denmark to specialise in it at DTU. My strengths were already on the mathematical side, so I was drawn to understanding how models actually work, not just using them. That gave me a profile that leans towards research and classical, mathematical AI, with signal processing from electrical engineering staying useful throughout, especially in my thesis on time-series and audio.

## How I approach problems

- I'm pragmatic and I like to take initiative. I don't like sitting in the unknown: I'm uncomfortable when I don't understand the reason behind something, so I dig until I do.
- I usually come up with my own solution first, then bring it to the team to discuss. Different perspectives almost always lead to a better result than any one person's idea.
- I enjoy the innovative side of development: finding more efficient alternatives to existing solutions, keeping up with new techniques, and looking for optimisations that give real improvements.
- Innovation courses at DTU shaped how I go from problem to solution: brainstorm, conceptualise, validate with tools like small comparative usability tests, then pitch. Sustainability was a consistent part of that, and it's something I care about.
- I'm comfortable taking on topics I don't know yet. My thesis was in explainable AI for time-series, a young research field I hadn't worked in before, with no fixed goal at the start.

## Working with a team

I've played team sports my whole life, so team dynamics come naturally to me. I'm at my best in cooperative settings, and I like bouncing ideas off people, especially more experienced colleagues, because that's often where the best outcomes come from. I can be reserved at first but warm up quickly, and I communicate easily once I'm settled.

I'm also motivated by gradually taking on responsibility: being trusted with tasks and the independence that comes with that is how I grow fastest.

## What I'm looking for

Early in my career, my priority is growth, both personal and professional. I want a team where I can learn from more experienced people, have room to experiment and some creative freedom, and still have fun along the way. A healthy balance between high standards and a sustainable work-life relationship matters a lot to me at this stage. I'm especially drawn to work with a clear purpose, like sustainability or healthcare, and to international teams: I'm not originally from Denmark and I'm comfortable in global environments.

## Why sports and data

I've always been fascinated by statistics and numbers, mostly because of sport: I keep wondering how data from real games could be used to improve performance. Flag football has very little data or analytics today, which is why I started Fudl, a computer vision project to collect that data automatically and eventually analyse games with machine learning.

- I'm curious and competitively driven, always looking for something to tackle.
